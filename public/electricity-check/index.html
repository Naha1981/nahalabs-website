Were you overcharged for electricity?

In November 2026 the energy regulator NERSA admitted in the High Court that the 2025/26 electricity tariffs it approved for five South African municipalities were unlawful. Answer three questions to see whether you may be affected.

What happened, in short

  • On 9-11 November 2026, in the High Court in Pretoria, NERSA conceded that the 2025/26 electricity tariffs it approved for Johannesburg, Ekurhuleni, Nelson Mandela Bay, Emfuleni and Madibeng were based on calculations containing errors, and were therefore approved unlawfully.
  • In Nelson Mandela Bay, the court heard that businesses were charged up to 76% more than the cost of supplying them. No such figure has been published yet for the other municipalities.
  • The court has not yet decided how customers will be compensated. It has raised appointing an independent expert to calculate the overcharges - so nobody can calculate an exact figure today.
  • This is a pattern: tariffs for 2023/24 were already set aside by the courts for Johannesburg, Ekurhuleni, Madibeng and Msunduzi, and NERSA has still not redetermined them.
  • Keep paying your current bill in full. Do not deduct amounts yourself - that risks disconnection.
